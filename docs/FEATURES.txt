PERSONAL FINANCIAL DASHBOARD - FEATURE LIST
===========================================

A single-user personal finance app (INR only). Spring Boot back end, file-based H2 database, plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript
front end. Everything runs on your own computer.


1. VIEWS (the tab row)
----------------------
- Calendar: one month at a time, money in (green) and out (red) for each day, a line with the month's In, Out and Net.
- Calendar markers: Ledger payments due, credit card bill days, diary notes (note icon), reminders (bell icon).
- Calendar day: click a day to see its transactions, Ledger payments, reminders and notes; add a reminder from it.
- Transactions: every transaction, newest first, with paging (10, 25, 50 or 100 a page) and an edit/delete pencil per row.
- Budgets: monthly spending limits with how much of each is used.
- Spending Review: the biggest expense and spending observations for each month.
- Stats: income and expense donuts by category, with their own period (Weekly, Monthly, Annually, Period).
- Accounts: coloured tiles, one per level; click a tile to see and add that level's accounts.
- Net worth: assets, debt and total, plus a month-end net worth graph.
- Reminders and Notes: reached from the sidebar (Organiser group), not from the tab row.
- One card that turns over to show the chosen view; the Calendar is shown first.


2. DATE RANGE
-------------
- A shared Today / From / To row scopes the Calendar, Transactions, Net worth and the line graphs.
- Clear button to remove the range.
- The Calendar jumps to the range's month and highlights its days.
- Stats has its own period buttons; Accounts, Budgets, Spending Review, Notes and Reminders are not scoped by dates.
- A round red + opens a new Expense (switch to Income or Transfer at the top of the form, date and amount are kept).


3. TRANSACTIONS
---------------
- Income, expense and transfer, with a note, a description, a category and sub category.
- A transfer can carry a fee.
- Account lists put the most used account first; the Note field suggests earlier notes of the same kind.
- Search box: words that must all appear in note, description, category, accounts, person, kind, date or amount.
- Filters: category (with its sub categories, or Uncategorised), account (either side), amount range, kind chips.
- A line of totals (found, in, out) while searching.
- Export CSV of everything listed (all pages), safe against spreadsheet formula injection.
- Jump to the matching list from an account row or from a Stats category.
- Columns: Date, Type, Category, Sub category, Note, Account, Amount (stacked on a narrow window).
- Tags: each transaction gets up to 10 tags worked out from its note and description, then its sub category, then its category;
  you can add, remove or re-suggest them in the form; they show as pills (a pill opens the list on that tag), a row of the most used
  tags with counts sits above the list like in Notes (click to narrow the list, click again to clear), they are found by the
  search box, and are in the CSV and Excel exports. Existing transactions were tagged once; Ledger, portfolio and jewellery
  transactions are tagged too.


4. ACCOUNTS
-----------
- Account types: cash, bank, digital wallet, goal fund, investment, gold, jewellery, property, vehicle, receivables,
  debt, credit card and other.
- Seven levels: Liquid money, Goals, Investments, Fixed assets, Money owed to you, Liabilities, Other.
- Balances are always calculated from the opening balance and the transactions, never stored.
- Debts and credit cards show the amount owed.
- Per-level "+ Add account" popup; edit, deactivate and delete accounts.
- Digital wallets (PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay): top up with a transfer, spend with an expense.
- Vehicles with number plate and year bought.
- A button on each account row jumps to its transactions.
- Safeguards: only one Receivables and one Jewellery account; an account cannot change between an asset and a debt, or into or
  out of Receivables, Jewellery or Credit Card (add a new account instead).


5. CREDIT CARDS AND LOANS YOU OWE
---------------------------------
- Credit card limit, usage bar, withdrawal (taken and not yet paid back) and credit still available.
- Optional statement day and bill due day (two different days; a short month uses its last day).
- Statement cycles: a fold under each card's row in the Accounts view shows one cycle at a time with ‹ dates › to page between
  cycles (it opens on the running one): bill status (paid, part paid, due, overdue), spent and paid totals, and the transactions
  in pages like the main list (10/25/50/100 a page) with chips All, Spent and Paid into the card. The main Transactions list is unchanged.
- A "remaining balance" line under the account of every transaction row (both accounts of a transfer): the account's balance just
  after that transaction; a loan shows what is still owed, a card the credit left on it. Not for Receivables, Jewellery, Mutual Funds or Shares.
- Pay button on each card and loan row: records a Liability Payments expense that lowers the amount owed.
- Fixed loans (a set monthly EMI, pre-filled in the Pay window) or custom loans (any amount, any time).


6. EMERGENCY FUND AND GOAL FUNDS
--------------------------------
- Emergency Fund with a target (months of essential expenses, or an amount), progress, runway and an estimated date.
- Goal funds with targets and progress.
- Ring-fencing: once a goal's target is reached, money can go in but nothing can come out until released.


7. FUTURE EXPENSES LEDGER
-------------------------
- One pot of money split into buckets for bills you know are coming (insurance, wifi, subscriptions).
- Fund it from a bank account, pay a bill from a bucket, credit interest, move money between buckets, edit and close buckets.
- A default Buffer bucket absorbs extra money, so nothing is ever unallocated.
- Every change is kept as history; next payment dates appear in the Calendar and in Coming up.


8. MUTUAL FUNDS AND SHARES
--------------------------
- Holdings with optional quantity, invested amount, current value, gain and gain percent, shown to the paisa.
- Invest from a bank or cash account, withdraw, update a value by hand.
- Dividends on shares, recorded as income.
- Each day's figures are saved to draw value-over-time graphs (Stats button).
- Allocation donut showing how the card's money is split between its holdings.


9. JEWELLERY
------------
- Gold, silver, platinum and diamond items kept as items (name, type, weight, price paid) in one Jewellery account.
- Add an item you already own, or one paid from a bank or cash account.
- Sell all or part of an item into a bank or cash account (the difference is recorded as income or an expense).
- Remove an item with no money moving; ordinary transactions on the account are refused.


10. LOANS YOU GAVE (RECEIVABLES)
--------------------------------
- People you have lent to, with Loans Given and Loans Collected as fixed categories tied to a person.
- Receivables is worked out from them; it is shown but not counted in net worth.
- Split an expense with people: tick "Split this expense with people" in the Expense form, choose people from the Receivables list (or
  add one on the spot) and share the bill equally (you are one of the members), by amount or by percent. The transaction keeps its full
  amount; each person's share is owed under their name in Receivables, which folds out into the expenses shared with them.
- Settle: the Settle button on a person records a payment (day, account, amount, optional note) and clears their oldest shared expenses
  first, or only the ones you tick; each expense shows what is settled and left, and each payment lists what it cleared.
- Stats, Budgets, Spending Review, the Calendar totals and the Transactions found-totals count a split expense at your own share only.
- When somebody else paid: tick the Split box (Paid by only appears then; otherwise every expense is yours) and choose them in "Paid by" and enter your share of the bill. No account moves; what you owe them is its
  own "You owe people" line (under the people in Receivables, and a grey line under net worth), never taken off Receivables or net worth.
- One balance per person (what they owe you, loans included, less what you owe them; negative when you owe them) and one Settle
  button beside the name: what you owe each other is set off first, the rest is paid by whoever owes (from them when they owe you,
  from you only when you owe more; not counted as lending). The Receivables total is that balance across everyone. The arrow before a name folds out their
  transactions: loans, shared expenses and bills they paid for you, what they owed before you started, any write-off (with its day and reason; change them with the pencil or take the write-off back with the bin), and the payments.


11. NET WORTH
-------------
- Figures as a sum: Assets, minus Debt, and the total; hidden until you press the eye.
- Fold-out of Assets by kind of account.
- Month-end graph as Line, Bar or Area; 1Y, 3Y, 5Y and All zoom; long stretches drawn per quarter.
- Summary line (movement, best and weakest month) and a table grouped by year.
- "Where your assets are" allocation ring on the back of the graph.
- History replays every transaction by month for up to five years (60 months).


12. STATS
---------
- Income and expense donuts by category (every category is its own slice), with a table that doubles as the legend.
- Click a category to see it month by month, with sub categories.
- Periods: Weekly, Monthly (default), Annually or any From and To, with arrows to move.


13. BUDGETS
-----------
- Monthly spending limit on all spending, and on any expense category, main or sub (for example Food, or Food > Dining out).
- A main category's limit counts its sub categories; a sub category's counts only itself, so both can be set.
- Each card shows used, left and a green, amber or red bar; ok under 80%, close from 80%, over above 100%.
- A main budget and its sub budgets are grouped and fold (open by itself when one needs attention).
- Month arrows to look at earlier months; the same limit repeats every month.
- Alerts: a Budgets panel in the Coming up bar and a warning mark on the tab for budgets that are close or over.
- Deleting a category removes its limit; limits appear on the Budgets sheet of the Excel export.


14. SPENDING REVIEW
-------------------
- Works by month with previous / next arrows.
- Biggest expense: the largest single expense of the month (amount, note or category, day).
- Transactions this month: the count, with expenses / income / transfers, the busiest day and the number of active days.
- Spending observations (one panel, three tinted panels inside): Spending increased, Spending decreased (categories that
  moved by at least 20% and Rs 500 against the month before; the running month against the same days) and Insight: frequent
  small expenses (categories with 5 or more expenses under Rs 500).
- Worked out in the browser; Loans Given and balance adjustments are not counted as spending.

15. REMINDERS AND "COMING UP"
-----------------------------
- Coming up bar with panels: Bills (card bills, statements, Ledger payments), Reminders, Recurring, Budgets, Done recently.
- Bills panel: card bills due within 7 days (using the statement when one exists), Ledger payments, late ones up to 30 days.
- Your own reminders: a day and a title, a tick box, late ones stay at the top, a Tomorrow button.
- Drag and drop: drag an open one-off reminder from one calendar day to another (titles show inside the day's cell), or from the day list
  or the Coming up bar onto a day; repeating and ticked reminders are not draggable.
- Repeating reminders (every week, month or year) with each occurrence tickable separately; birthdays show a years count.
- Done recently: reminders ticked in the last 7 days, still tickable to bring one back.
- Reminders view: ticked reminders grouped by month, a summary strip (this week, month, year, on time or late), search,
  year filter and paging.
- Clicking an item opens that day in the Calendar.


16. NOTES (DIARY)
-----------------
- Any number of notes for any day, with optional title and tags.
- Tags are worked out from the title and text (names, repeated words, words and phrases used in earlier note tags; at most four)
  while you type; add your own, remove any, or press Suggest to work them out again.
- Tag list with counts to narrow the list; search over text and tags; paged list.
- Notes appear on the Calendar day and are marked on the Calendar.
- Clicking a note opens it in a reading view (day, folder, title, full text, tags); its Edit button opens the form.


17. CATEGORIES
--------------
- Starter categories on first run (a short flat list), then add, rename, deactivate and delete your own.
- Two levels: categories and sub categories.
- Fixed categories (Loans Given, Loans Collected, Liability Payments) are protected from edits.


18. SIDE MENU
-------------
- Collapsible sidebar; the hamburger button folds it to a slim bar or opens it; the choice is remembered.
- Starts folded on a narrow window.
- Items nested under five groups; pointing at a group opens its items in a flyout beside it (no click needed): Organiser (Reminders, Notes), Categories (Edit / Add categories),
  Themes (Light, Dark, Color), Data (Backups, Export to Excel), Account (Screen lock, Change password).
- The pressed item shows which Organiser view is open.


19. THEMES AND MOTION
---------------------
- Three themes: Light, Dark and Color (teal accent, mint-to-peach page); remembered in the browser.
- Until you choose, Light or Dark follows the computer; applied before anything is drawn (no flash).
- Chart colours checked for contrast and colour-blind separation.
- Motion: fade-ins, press feedback, rising rows, ring sweeps, count-up figures, sliding bars; none of it when the computer
  is set to reduce motion.


20. FIRST-RUN SETUP
-------------------
- Setup wizard on first run: choose a password, then add your accounts level by level.
- Starter categories loaded automatically.


21. SECURITY
------------
- One user (admin) over HTTP Basic; the password is set in the app and stored only as a BCrypt hash.
- Screen lock covers the page when it opens, after 15 minutes idle, and from the menu button.
- Wrong-password throttle (10 failures, then a 30 second lock).
- Server listens on 127.0.0.1 only; requests from other host names are refused.
- Changes to the API need the X-Requested-With header (cross-site protection).
- Forgot the password: start with --reset-password (data is untouched).


22. BACKUPS
-----------
- Automatic checked backups: at start, at close and daily; each verified against the live data before it is kept.
- Keeps the last 30 days plus the first backup of every month; optional second folder (iCloud, Dropbox, USB).
- Red warning bar when a backup fails or is old; yellow bar while backups are only on this computer (can be hidden).
- Backups popup showing status; restore with --restore=FILE (the old database is moved aside, never deleted).


23. EXCEL EXPORT
----------------
- One button downloads a workbook with sheets for accounts, transactions, income and expenses by category, monthly totals,
  net worth, Ledger and entries, Emergency Fund, Mutual Funds, Shares, Jewellery, loans, categories, budgets, notes,
  reminders and holding history.
- Native Excel charts next to their tables.


24. LOGGING AND DIAGNOSTICS
---------------------------
- Server log by day under logs/, with request logging and change logging by id (never amounts, names or text).
- The page also logs errors and start-up problems to the server log.


25. RUNNING AND DEMO DATA
-------------------------
- scripts/financial-dashboard.sh starts the app (and an optional local Caddy proxy for http://finance.localhost/).
- scripts/check-setup.sh checks which tools are installed.
- Demo database: scripts/financial-dashboard.sh demo, then scripts/load-demo-data.py loads five years of data
  (accounts, thousands of transactions, funds, loans, Ledger, notes, reminders, portfolios, jewellery, sub categories,
  budgets and stand-outs for Spending Review).
- Database changes are applied by Flyway migrations; existing databases are never rebuilt.
- Tests: Java service and API tests, plus JavaScript tests for the front end (npm-free, Node's built-in runner).
