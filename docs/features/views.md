# The dashboard: views

What each tab shows, and how the shared date range and the red + work.

[← Back to the README](../../README.md) · [All features](../FEATURES.txt)

Under the header there is a row of seven buttons and one card below it that turns over to show the view you choose (Reminders and Notes are in the menu, under Organiser). It
opens on the Calendar.

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | One month at a time. Each day shows money in (green) and out (red), transfers left out. A first line gives the month's In, Out and Net. Days show their exact money in and out (no rounding, for example +₹1,30,000.50). Days with a Ledger payment due carry a marker, and days with a diary note carry a 📝. Click a day to see its transactions, its Ledger payments and its notes. |
| **Transactions** | Every transaction, newest first, with a **search box** (words that must all appear in the note, description, category, accounts, person, kind, date or amount, for example `shop food 2,400`), filters for **category** (with its sub categories, or Uncategorised), **account** (either side) and an **amount range**, the Income / Expense / Transfer chips and the shared date range. While you search it says how many it found and what came in and went out. Each row reads Date, Type, Category, Sub category, Note, Account and Amount in its own column (stacked on a narrow window). Paging (10, 25, 50 or 100 a page) and an edit/delete pencil on each row. **Export CSV** downloads everything listed (all pages) for Excel, with a Tags column; the 🧾 button on an account, and **View transactions** inside a Stats category, jump here with the matching filters and period. |
| **Budgets** | Monthly spending limits ([Budgets](budgets-and-review.md)): one on all spending and one per expense category, main or sub, each with a bar for how much of this month's limit is used, what is left, and a green, amber or red state. Related budgets (a category and its sub categories) fold together; ‹ › show earlier months; **+ Budget** adds one. |
| **Spending Review** | The biggest expense of a month and spending observations (categories that rose or fell against the month before, frequent small expenses), with ‹ › to move between months. |
| **Stats** | A donut for income and one for expenses by category (every category is its own slice, biggest first), each with a table that doubles as the legend. Buttons choose the period: **Weekly, Monthly (the default), Annually or Period** (any From and To). Weekly, Monthly and Annually show one week, month or year with ‹ › arrows to the ones before and after. Click a category to see it month by month. |
| **Accounts** | Colourful tiles, one per level (Liquid money, Goals, Investments, Fixed assets, Money owed to you, Liabilities, Other), each with its total and account count. Click a tile to see that level's accounts and add new ones. |
| **Net worth** | The figures as a sum in bold (Assets, − Debt, and the bold total under a line; hidden until you press the eye; Receivables, money you have lent, is shown below as "not counted" because it is yours but not with you yet; an arrow under Assets opens the lines it is made of (hidden to start with): cash, bank accounts, goal funds, investments, gold, property, vehicles), and a month-end net worth graph as a Line, Bar or Area chart. It opens on the last 12 months; **1Y, 3Y, 5Y and All** buttons show more history (a long stretch is drawn as one point per quarter), a summary line says how far net worth moved and the best and weakest month, and the table is grouped by year. The graph draws itself each time you open the view or change the zoom or chart type. A note under the figures says that Assets, Debt, the kinds of account and the ring show today's values, not the date range or the history. Two buttons above the graph, **Graph** and **Allocation**, turn the card over: on its back is a donut, **Where your assets are**, splits what you own by kind of account (every kind in its own slice, biggest first, with no Other) with a table of amounts and shares; amounts follow the eye, the shares always show, and debt and money lent are not in it. |
| **Notes** | (Menu > Organiser.) A diary: any number of notes for any day, with **tags** the app works out from the title and text (the meaningful words, names written with capitals, and words or phrases you have used as note tags before, up to four; in a long note only words used more than once) that you can add to, remove or re-suggest with **↻ Suggest**, searchable. A row of your tags (with counts) above the list narrows it to one tag. The list is paged (10, 25 or 50 a page). Clicking a note opens it in a reading view; its **Edit** button opens the form. |
| **Reminders** | (Menu > Organiser.) Every one-off reminder you have ticked, and every ticked day of a repeating one, grouped by the month it was ticked in, newest first (the newest month open, the others folded). A strip at the top counts what you finished this week, this month and this year and how many were on time or late; a search box and a Year list narrow the rows, and the list is paged (10, 25, 50 or 100 rows a page, 25 to start). A row has its tick box (untick to bring it back), the day it was for, the day it was done, a red "N days late" when it was ticked after its day, and a 🗑️. Clicking the title opens its day in the Calendar. |

A shared **Today / From / To** row scopes the Calendar, Transactions, Net worth and the line graphs to a date range
(Stats has its own period buttons; Budgets and Spending Review work by month with their own ‹ ›; Accounts, Notes and Reminders are not scoped by dates). A red round **+** at its right opens a new
**Expense**, with **Income** on its left and **Transfer** on its right across the top of the form to switch (the date and
amount you typed are kept); it is not shown on Stats, Net worth, Accounts, Budgets, Spending Review, Notes or Reminders.
