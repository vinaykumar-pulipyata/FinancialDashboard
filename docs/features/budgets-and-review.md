# Budgets, Spending Review and heatmaps

Monthly spending limits, what changed this month, and the Calendar heatmaps.

[← Back to the README](../../README.md) · [All features](../FEATURES.txt)

- **Budgets.** The 🎯 Budgets view sets a **monthly spending limit** on all your spending and on any expense category, main or sub (Food, Travel, Shopping, or Food › Dining out), with **+ Budget** (the pencil on a card changes or deletes one; setting a limit that exists replaces it). A main category's budget and the budgets of its sub categories are **together in one group that folds** (▸ / ▾; it opens by itself while one of them is close or over, and what you open or fold is kept while the page stays open). Each card shows how much of the limit this month has used, with a green, amber or red bar and what is left. A limit repeats every month, a main category's limit includes its sub categories while a sub category's counts only itself (so both can be set), and Loans Given are not spending. ‹ › show earlier months. From **80% used** a budget is "close" and above 100% it is "over": the Coming up bar then gets a **Budgets** panel (a row opens the view) and the Budgets tab carries a ⚠ mark. The limits are kept by the server; the sums are done in the browser from your transactions. Deleting a category removes its limit.

- **Spending Review.** The 💡 Spending Review view works by month (‹ › move between months, never past the current one or before the first expense) and has three parts. **Biggest expense** and **Transactions this month** sit side by side: the largest single expense (amount, note or category, day) and the number of transactions in the month with a count of expenses, income and transfers, the busiest day and the days with any. **Spending observations** is one panel with three tinted panels inside: *Spending increased* and *Spending decreased* (categories that moved by at least 20% and Rs 500 against the month before, the running month against the same days of it; a category new this month counts from Rs 1,000) and *Insight: frequent small expenses* (categories with 5 or more expenses under Rs 500, at most 3). Worked out in the browser from the loaded transactions (`js/panels/insights-calc.js`); Loans Given and balance adjustments are not counted as spending.

- **Calendar heatmaps**: above the calendar, **Calendar | Expenses | Income** switches the same grid between its usual view and two heatmaps. In **Expenses** each day is filled red and in **Income** green, in five steps (none, very low, low, high, very high) that follow the month's own days: a day's step is where it stands among the days that had activity, so one huge day does not make the rest look like nothing, and a day with nothing is grey. Each day still shows its amount, hovering, tapping or tabbing to it shows its date, total, number of transactions and average, and the arrow keys move between days. A line above the grid gives the month's total, the days with and without activity, the highest day and the average per active day; future days are neutral and not counted as quiet. It counts what Stats counts (transfers, Loans Given and Collected and balance adjustments are left out; a foreign-currency amount is in rupees at the rate of its day). The month and the chosen day stay when you switch; the chosen day's list has a link that opens Transactions on that day and kind.
