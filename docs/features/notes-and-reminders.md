# Notes, reminders and the Coming up bar

The private diary, note folders, your own reminders and the bills that are coming up.

[← Back to the README](../../README.md) · [All features](../FEATURES.txt)

- **Private diary**: Daily Calendar Notes are encrypted in the database (and so in backups) with a key made from your password, and are shown as dots on screen. Click one (a note opens in a reading view; its **Edit** button opens the form), or **🔒 Diary hidden · Show** in the Notes folder row, and type your password to read them for 5 minutes (or until Hide, the screen lock or a reload). While hidden, search and tags do not look inside the diary. Changing the password keeps the diary readable. **If you forget the password and reset it, the diary cannot be read with a new password**: it is not deleted, and setting the old password again opens it. Notes in your own folders are ordinary text. The day of a note stays visible so the Calendar can mark it.

- **Note folders**: Notes has a row of folders: All notes, Daily Calendar Notes (your private diary, the notes with no folder) and any folders you make with + Folder (Recipes, Study Materials...). Pick one to list only its notes (search and tags work inside it); Rename folder and Delete folder show for a chosen folder, and only an empty folder can be deleted. The note window has a Folder choice and a new note starts in the folder on show. Every note still has a day and shows on the Calendar.

- **Coming up.** Up to three panels just under the page title, side by side (stacked on a narrow window; an empty panel is left out):
  **Bills** lists credit card bills (when a due day is set and the card owes something; with a statement day, only once that statement exists and has something left to pay) and Ledger payments due within
  the next 7 days, and Ledger bills that are late (up to 30 days), with the amount and the day; **Reminders** lists your
  own one-off **reminders** (see Reminders below) with a tick box until they are done, and **Recurring** lists your repeating ones from
  7 days before their day. A fourth panel, **Done recently**, keeps the reminders you ticked in the last 7 days (only ones that are themselves from the last 7 days, the latest 5, newest first, with a note for any more) with their tick box still ticked, so one ticked by mistake comes back by unticking it. Each panel has a count, a late item is red and today's is bold. Clicking an item opens that
  day in the Calendar, which also marks each card's bill day.

- **Reminders.** Your own dated reminders for anything ("renew car insurance", "call the CA"). Choose a day in the Calendar
  and press **+ Add reminder**; days with one still to do carry a 🔔. Tick the box to mark one done (in the day or in the
  Coming up bar; untick to reopen it). A reminder that is not ticked by its day is **late**: it stays in the bar, at the top,
  until you tick it, move it or delete it. A late one has a **Tomorrow** button, **drag a reminder onto another day** to move it: take its title straight from one day's cell to another (a cell shows up to two open one-off reminders as small draggable titles, then "+N more"), or from the day list below the Calendar, or from the Coming up bar, or click it to change its day or text. Set **Repeat** to every week, every month or every year for a birthday, an anniversary or the rent: choose its first day (the birth date, the first rent day) and it comes back by itself, on the Calendar (🔔) and in the Coming up bar (🔁) for the week before and the day itself. A yearly one shows the count ("35 years"). A repeating reminder is never late and cannot be dragged, and it is never done as a whole, but **each day it falls on can be ticked** (in the Calendar day, in Recurring, or in Done recently): ticking the rent for 5 November does not touch 5 December, and the next day takes over in Recurring. A day you never tick simply drops out after it passes: it is not shown as late. Ticked days appear in the Reminders view with a 🔁. A monthly one on the 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month, and 29 February falls on the 28th in other years. A **🗑️** button on each reminder in the day deletes it (after asking), for a wrong entry. Reminders only show while the app is open in the browser: there
  are no phone alerts or emails.
